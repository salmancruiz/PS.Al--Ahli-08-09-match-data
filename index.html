Coordinate & chart methodology

Pitch geometry uses a 105 × 68 plotting frame inferred from the supplied shot-coordinate ranges. Shot coordinates are displayed in a shared attacking direction for the half-pitch. On the full-pitch, Al Ahli coordinates are rotated 180° so opponents attack opposite goals. Lineup layout coordinates are tactical placements, not tracking data. Marker size is scaled by xG; ★ identifies a recorded goal. The xG chart uses cumulative shot xG in recorded event order.

Known source inconsistency

Heatmap data availability

A heatmapUrl endpoint is referenced but no movement/touch heatmap point arrays are embedded. That endpoint has not been fetched. The included density panel is explicitly limited to recorded shot origins.
